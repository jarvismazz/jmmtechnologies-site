This website

JMM Technologies LLC operates this site. We do not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, contact-form services, or tracking cookies. Fonts and product images are served with the site.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

When you contact us

Email and telephone links open your own email or calling app. If you contact us, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address, message, and attachments. We use it to respond, investigate issues, and maintain relevant support records. Email and communications providers process those messages to deliver the service.

Please share only what is needed. You can ask us to review or delete support correspondence; any applicable recordkeeping obligations may limit deletion. We do not sell support messages or use them for advertising.
