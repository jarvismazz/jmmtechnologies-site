Stack Em! Support

Stack Em! is an offline block puzzle for iPhone. Place the available pieces, complete rows or columns to clear space, and keep the board open as you chase a higher score.

Need help?

Email john@jmmtechnologies.app and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot is helpful when the issue is visual.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm you are running the latest version of Stack Em!.
  • Close and reopen the app.
  • Restart your iPhone if sound, haptics, or display behavior seems stuck.
