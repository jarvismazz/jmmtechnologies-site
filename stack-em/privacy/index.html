Stack Em! Privacy Policy

Effective August 5, 2026

Stack Em! version 1.0 is an offline puzzle game published by JMM Technologies LLC. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.

Information we collect

JMM Technologies does not collect personal information through Stack Em! version 1.0. The app does not transmit gameplay data to JMM Technologies or to third-party services.

Information stored on your device

Stack Em! stores game preferences and progress, such as settings, score, experience, and selected theme, locally on your device using Apple platform storage. This information is not sent to JMM Technologies. Removing the app may remove this locally stored information.

Children's privacy

Because version 1.0 does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds online services, advertising, analytics, or other data practices, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to john@jmmtechnologies.app.
