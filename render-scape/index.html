Before you buy

Know your Mac.

Apple Silicon for live animation.

Live animation requires an Apple Silicon Mac. Intel Macs show one bundled still image, not the configurable live scenes.

Render Scape requires macOS 13 or later.

Motion pauses on battery.

When plugged in, animation resumes unless power, accessibility or safety conditions keep it paused. Low Power Mode, Reduce Motion and other safety conditions can also hold a still frame. Eco reduces the animation target when plugged in.

Your desktop, not your lock screen.

Render Scape is a desktop wallpaper app, not a lock-screen or screensaver app. Quitting it reveals your existing macOS wallpaper.

Some scenes combine live effects with still artwork; imported photos remain still.
