Choose your atmosphere

  1. Open Render Scape from its menu-bar item.
  2. Choose a scene in Wallpapers.
  3. Open Customize to tune it. Changes save automatically.
  4. Use Rotation to select scenes or still images and a switching interval.
  5. Use Pause/Resume to control motion.

Understand power behavior

Live rendering requires Apple Silicon. Animation pauses on battery; eligible AC power allows it to resume. Eco is optional and reduces the target frame rate. Reduce Motion and thermal safeguards can also hold a still image.

Close, or quit?

Closing Settings leaves the wallpaper running. Choose Quit Render Scape from the menu to stop it and reveal your macOS wallpaper.
